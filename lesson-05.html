Why Workflow Matters

Professional editing is more than correcting sentences. An editor needs a repeatable process that keeps the project organized from the first conversation with the author to the final delivery.

A strong workflow helps you understand the assignment, identify what the manuscript needs, work systematically, communicate clearly with the author, and perform a final quality check before delivery.

The exact workflow may change depending on the project and type of editing, but the principle remains the same: know what you are doing before you start doing it.
